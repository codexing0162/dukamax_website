# QUICK START - Google OAuth Verification (5 Minutes)

## 🚀 What You Need to Do RIGHT NOW

### Step 1: Get Verification Code (2 minutes)
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Enter: `https://dukamax.live`
4. Choose DNS TXT verification (fastest)
5. **Copy the TXT record value** that looks like:
   ```
   v=google-site-verification=XXXXXXXXXXXXXX
   ```

### Step 2: Add to Your Domain DNS (2 minutes)
1. Log into where you bought dukamax.live (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)
2. Find DNS Settings
3. Add new TXT record:
   - **Name:** `@` (or leave empty)
   - **Type:** TXT
   - **Value:** Paste from Step 1

### Step 3: Verify (1 minute)
1. Wait 5-10 minutes
2. Go back to Google Search Console
3. Click "Verify"
4. See checkmark ✓

### Step 4: Resubmit to Google (0 minutes now, 1-2 days wait)
1. Go to Google Cloud Console
2. Click OAuth consent screen
3. Find "Request Verification" button
4. Click it
5. Wait 1-2 business days
