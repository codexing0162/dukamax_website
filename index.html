✨ Features

Everything You Need to Run Your Business

From quick sales to deep analytics, DukaMax gives you the tools to manage every aspect of your retail operation.

⚡

Lightning-Fast POS

Process sales in seconds with barcode scanning, quick product search, and intuitive checkout. Supports cash, M-Pesa, card, and split payments.

📦

Smart Inventory

Track stock levels in real-time with low stock alerts, expiry tracking, restock management, and support for both bulk and piece units.

👥

Customer & Debt Management

Keep track of every customer, manage debts and payments, and build stronger relationships with your buyers.

📊

Reports & Analytics

Beautiful charts and detailed reports — sales trends, top products, stock value, profit analysis, and exportable PDF reports.

📱

SMS Notifications

Send sale receipts via SMS, daily reports to the boss, and keep customers informed automatically.

☁️

Cloud Backup

Secure your data with Google Drive backup. Sync sales, products, and customer data for easy access anywhere.

🚀 How It Works

Set Up in Minutes, Sell in Seconds

DukaMax is designed for simplicity. No internet required for daily operations — your data stays on your device, always accessible.

✓

Install & Create Account

Download from Play Store, create your admin account, and configure your shop details in settings.

✓

Add Your Products

Import or manually add products with prices, categories, stock levels, expiry dates, and barcode info.

✓

Start Selling

Use the POS screen to scan or search products, process payments, and generate receipts — offline or online.

✓

Track & Grow

Monitor sales trends, manage expenses, handle debts, and make data-driven decisions with real-time analytics.

Advanced Features for Serious Business

Every tool you need to manage products, expenses, orders, and team members in one place.

Product Categories

Organize products into custom categories with icons. Filter, search, and manage your inventory by category for faster operations.

💸

Expense Tracking

Record daily business expenses by category. Track spending patterns and see how expenses impact your net profit.

Order Management

Handle customer orders with status tracking — from pending to completed. Keep a clear record of every order.

We believe your business data belongs to you — and only you. DukaMax is designed with privacy and security at its core.
