2. Description of the Service

DukaMax POS is a mobile point of sale and business management application that provides the following features:

  • Point of Sale (POS): Processing sales, generating receipts, barcode scanning, and supporting multiple payment methods (cash, M-Pesa, card, split payments).
  • Inventory Management: Tracking product stock, managing categories, low stock alerts, expiry tracking, and restock records.
  • Customer Management: Storing customer information, tracking purchases, and managing debt records.
  • Financial Reporting: Sales trends, profit analysis, expense tracking, and exportable PDF reports.
  • SMS Notifications: Sending sale receipts and daily reports via SMS.
  • Cloud Backup: Optional backup to Google Drive and data sync to Google.
  • Multi-User Support: Admin and Cashier roles with different permission levels.
  • Multi-Language: English and Swahili language support.
